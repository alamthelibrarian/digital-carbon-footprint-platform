# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in the Digital Carbon Footprint Platform (DCFP) a respectful, constructive, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or viewpoint.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Critique ideas, evidence, and code rather than people.
- Welcome questions and explain technical or methodological decisions clearly.
- Acknowledge uncertainty and correct errors transparently.
- Respect intellectual property, licenses, privacy, and confidentiality.
- Keep discussions focused on improving the project and its evidence base.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, doxxing, and publishing private or confidential information without permission are not acceptable.

## Scope

This code applies in project repositories, issue and pull-request discussions, and other spaces where someone is representing the project.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainer through the contact options on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Do not use public issues for sensitive reports.

## Enforcement

The project maintainer may edit or remove inappropriate content, close discussions, or restrict participation where necessary to protect a safe and constructive project environment. Decisions should be proportionate to the behaviour and applied consistently.
